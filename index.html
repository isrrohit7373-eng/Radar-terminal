3. Strict 3-State Dot Scoring Engine

  • ○ GRAY (AWAITING): Future completion leg (e.g. Point D / Breakout) not yet formed.

4. RSI + MA, MACD & Divergence Rules

  • RSI (14) & RSI MA (14): Wilder's Relative Strength Index smoothed with a 14-period SMA. RSI > RSI MA = bullish momentum; RSI < RSI MA = bearish momentum.
  • MACD (12, 26, 9): MACD = EMA(12) - EMA(26), Signal = EMA(9 of MACD), Histogram = MACD - Signal.
  • ● Bullish RSI Divergence: Price makes a Lower Low while RSI makes a Higher Low (plotted on Main Chart & RSI Pane).
  • ● Bearish RSI Divergence: Price makes a Higher High while RSI makes a Lower High.
  • ● Volume-Price Divergence: Price makes a new swing high/low on declining volume, signaling trend exhaustion and potential reversal.

5. OBI 5-Period Smoothing, VWAP Filter & Smart Action Matrix

  • OBI (5-Period Smoothed): Calculates per-candle Order Book / Order-Flow Imbalance ((Buy - Sell) / (Buy + Sell)) × 100 across the last 5 candles for each timeframe, plus separate Positive and Negative burst averages.
  • Multi-TF VWAP Alignment: Confirms whether price is above/below VWAP and aligned with EMA(20) vs EMA(50) across 1m, 5m, 15m, and 4h.
  • Smart Action Matrix Score (-100 to +100): Equally blends 4 pillars (25 pts each): OBI Pressure (70% 5-Avg + 30% Instant), VWAP & EMA Trend, RSI/MACD Momentum + Divergence, and Qualified Pattern Count × Avg Match Strength.
  • Verdicts: ≥ +45 = STRONG BUY · +18 to +44 = BUY · -17 to +17 = WAIT · -18 to -44 = SELL · ≤ -45 = STRONG SELL.
  • Average Score Columns — AVG SCORE (5) & AVG SCORE (14): Computes the rolling mean of the Smart Action Score across the last 5 candles (short-term momentum stability) and last 14 candles (sustained trend conviction) using live Binance historical candles for each timeframe.
